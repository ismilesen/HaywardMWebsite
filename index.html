Our Masjid

A place of worship, knowledge, and community

Masjid As-Salafi serves Muslims in Hayward and across the Bay Area. Our goal is to provide a welcoming space centered on the Qur'an and Sunnah, congregational prayer, beneficial knowledge, and service to the local community.
