Our Campaign

A center of worship, learning, and community

As-salamu alaykum wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh.

We are working to establish Masjid As-Salafi, a dedicated masjid for Muslims across the East Bay. It will welcome every Muslim while adhering to the Qur'an and authentic Sunnah.

By Allah's permission, the masjid will be a place where families pray, students seek beneficial knowledge, new Muslims find support, and future generations grow closer to Allah.
